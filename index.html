02TERRAIN PLANNING

Heightmap Wavefront Planner

A standalone ROS2 terrain-aware global planner over FastDEM elevation maps. It evaluates observed support, local PCA slope/roughness, step discontinuities and unknown regions before constructing a sampled roadmap.

Compared LiDAR-only scan matching and LiDAR-inertial localization for legged navigation. GenZ-ICP is retained as a scan-to-map research path while FAST-LIO is the practical backbone candidate for vibration-heavy real-robot operation.
