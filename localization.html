IMPLEMENTATION DETAILS

Algorithms live here, not on the front page.

FAST-LIO

LiDAR-inertial localization backbone candidate for real-robot navigation. The integration work focuses on odometry frames, TF connectivity, timestamps, and stable output for Nav2.

  • LiDAR + IMU
  • camera_init → body odometry semantics
  • Live / rosbag time-source policy

GenZ-ICP

LiDAR-only scan-matching path studied as a map-correction and localization candidate. Real-robot trials exposed sensitivity to legged-robot vibration and motion, motivating a LiDAR-inertial backbone.

  • Scan-to-map ICP
  • map → odom correction
  • Timestamp-aware TF publication
